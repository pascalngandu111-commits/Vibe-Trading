# TradeCoreFX authoritative roadmap

Locked 2026-08-15. This is the first authoritative individual definition of all 15 phases. It does not rewrite repository history: earlier documents described only Phases 1–3, Phases 1–5 collectively, and Phases 6–9 collectively. Status is engineering/evidence-specific and never implies profitability or deployment acceptance.

| Phase | Objective | Engineering acceptance gate | Deployment or evidence gate | Current status | Explicit non-goals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand and layout foundation. | Responsive brand/layout components and regressions pass. | Maintainer visual review. | ENGINEERING_COMPLETE | Product claims, analysis, execution. |
| 2 | Public homepage and product explanation. | Public routes, content and responsive tests pass. | Published-site content review. | ENGINEERING_COMPLETE | Authenticated workflows, profitability claims. |
| 3 | Authenticated application workspace. | Auth boundaries, navigation and workspace tests pass. | Target authentication/deployment review. | FOUNDATION_COMPLETE | Provider or production acceptance. |
| 4 | Risk Manager specification and deterministic calculation validation. | Calculation, validation and safety tests pass. | Independent financial-method review. | ENGINEERING_COMPLETE | Advice, guarantees, order sizing execution. |
| 5 | Performance Lab, journal persistence and evidence boundaries. | Persistence, calculations and evidence disclosures pass. | Research-method and retention review. | ENGINEERING_COMPLETE | Demonstrated performance or investor readiness. |
| 6 | Investor-selected FX pair analysis workflow. | Authenticated fixed-preset pair creation, UI and race tests pass. | Usability review with provider availability in target environment. | ENGINEERING_COMPLETE | Caller-selected presets/evidence, orders, directional preclaims. |
| 7 | Trusted market-provider and macro-evidence contracts. | Default-deny provenance and recorded failure fixtures pass deterministically; no credentialed provider harness is implemented. | Implement or supply an approved credentialed FX adapter, then complete current-provider and authorized macro connector acceptance externally. | EXTERNAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED | Fixtures as live evidence; model/caller self-trust. |
| 8 | Accessibility, deployment-security and operational acceptance harnesses. | Bounded concurrency/recovery/security/accessibility harnesses pass. | Manual browser, screen-reader, proxy, security and operational review. | EXTERNAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED | Fabricated deployment results; unattended default soak. |
| 9 | Beta release evidence review and acceptance decision. | Evidence checklist and decision record are complete. | Named reviewers approve or reject beta release. | NOT_STARTED | Automatic release based on engineering fixtures. |
| 10 | Historical calibration and walk-forward evaluation. | Reproducible leakage-controlled calibration suite passes. | Independent methodology/evidence review. | NOT_STARTED | Profitability promises or cherry-picked periods. |
| 11 | Portfolio exposure, correlation, concentration and drawdown controls. | Deterministic portfolio risk limits and regressions pass. | Risk-policy owner acceptance. | NOT_STARTED | Broker execution or guaranteed loss prevention. |
| 12 | Investor beta evidence package and controlled feedback protocol. | Versioned evidence package and consented feedback process exist. | Authorized beta cohort and governance approval. | NOT_STARTED | Public solicitation or performance claims. |
| 13 | Provider resilience, monitoring, backup, recovery and incident response. | Failover, alerting, restore and incident drills are testable. | Target-environment drills meet approved objectives. | NOT_STARTED | Assuming fixture recovery proves production recovery. |
| 14 | Authentication, privacy, legal, licensing and subscription acceptance. | Security/privacy/licensing inventories and subscription controls pass. | Legal, privacy, security and licensing sign-off. | NOT_STARTED | Legal conclusions by automated tests. |
| 15 | Controlled production release, rollback and post-release monitoring. | Release/rollback automation and safety checks pass. | Authorized release decision plus monitored rollout. | NOT_STARTED | Uncontrolled launch, broker execution, guarantees. |

`ENGINEERING_COMPLETE` means the repository implementation and deterministic tests meet the phase engineering gate. `FOUNDATION_COMPLETE` means the base exists but later acceptance remains. `PARTIAL` means only some named engineering work exists. `NOT_STARTED` means no phase acceptance claim. `EXTERNAL_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED` means engineering contracts/harnesses exist but credentialed, manual, legal, or target-environment evidence is still required.
